THE DETAILS

Questions, answered.

Can I paste a TikTok, YouTube or Facebook share link?

This starter version does not extract videos from platform share pages. Platform-specific support requires official or otherwise authorized integrations and must follow each platform's terms and copyright rules.

Why might a direct link not download?

The host may block cross-origin downloads, require authentication, or stream the media in a way that is not exposed as a direct file. Use the source website's official download option.

Do you upload my selected video?

No. When you choose a local file, the browser creates a local download. The file is not sent to the NexaSave server.
